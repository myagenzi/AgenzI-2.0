# The AI Audit

We go inside your business and come back with findings you could hand to anyone.

**What we look at**

**Where the hours go.** Mapped by function. Not the org chart — the actual week.

**What repeats.** Anything done more than twice a week the same way is a candidate.

**Whether your data is ready.** Most AI projects fail here, before anything is built. If it's a mess, that's the finding.

**What you already own.** Tools, licences, usage. Most businesses pay for things nobody opens.

**What you get**

Findings, a verdict, and a sequence.

The verdict is one of three: **build something**, **fix what's there**, or **teach your people**. Sometimes it's "none of these yet" — and if that's the answer, that's what we'll write.

6–10 pages, plus a one-page summary for anyone who needs the short version.

# SHARED SPECIFICATIONS

## Every form needs

☐ Working email delivery — **test all three end to end before launch**
☐ Privacy Policy link in the consent line
☐ Spam protection (honeypot field, not a CAPTCHA — CAPTCHAs cost conversions)
☐ Mobile-tested, every field
☐ Inline validation, not on-submit
☐ Submissions stored somewhere retrievable, not email-only
☐ Confirmation screen, not just a toast message

## Response time promises made

| Form | Promised | Must be met |
|---|---|---|
| Free Report | 2 working days | The report itself |
| Audit | 1 working day | Invoice + call times |
| Buy Minutes | 1 working day | Invoice + estimate + cap |

⚠️ **These are the first promise AgenzI makes to anyone.** Missing them undoes everything the copy claims about speed. If two working days isn't realistic with your workload, change the number on the form before launch rather than missing it in week one.

## Data handling — needed for the Privacy Policy

**Collected:** name, work email, company, website, business process descriptions, tool usage.
**Stored:** [form tool] → [where].
**Shared with:** [name every third party — form provider, email provider, any AI tool used to help draft reports].
**Retention:** [decide — 24 months is a reasonable default].
**Deletion on request:** [email address].

⚠️ **If you use an AI tool to help write the free reports, client business information passes through that vendor.** This must be disclosed in the Privacy Policy. Canadian buyers will ask.

## Slot counter

The audit page says *"[X] launch slots remaining."* This must be real and it must decrement. A counter that never moves is worse than no counter — and a launch offer with no cap is how your only junior's month disappears for $500.

Suggested: **10 slots.** Manual update is fine at this volume.
